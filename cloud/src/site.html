LyricDock

A phone on your desk, or any browser, that becomes a Spotify lyrics dock: word-synced lyrics, the album art and playback controls for whatever you play.

Open the web app · Source on GitHub

Signing in

The apps use Sign in with Google only to let you in. LyricDock receives your name, email address and profile picture, keeps them only on your device (a sign-in cookie in your browser, a sign-in token in the app), and never stores or shares them. Your Spotify sign-in stays on your device. Details: privacy policy and terms of service.

Cover API

Base URL https://art.lyricdock.losthusky.qzz.io. Album details and Apple Music’s animated covers, looked up once and indexed. For the LyricDock apps: every call needs a LyricDock sign-in (Authorization: Bearer app token).

GET /v1/coverartist, album, px (size needed), hevc=1, shape=square|tall, q=max → still image + the best-fitting animated cover video
GET /v1/healthis it up
curl -H "Authorization: Bearer $TOKEN" "https://art.lyricdock.losthusky.qzz.io/v1/cover?artist=Gorillaz&album=Plastic%20Beach&px=1080&d=$DEVICE"
