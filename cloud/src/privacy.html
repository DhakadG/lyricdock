LyricDock privacy policy

Effective 8 October 2026.

LyricDock shows word-synced lyrics, album art and playback controls for what you play on Spotify, on a phone, a tablet or in a web browser. It is a personal, non-commercial project by Ghanishth Kumawat. This page explains what LyricDock collects, why, and what you can do about it. Questions: ghanisht.kumawat@gmail.com.

The short version

  • LyricDock does not sell, rent or share your data, show ads, or build profiles of you.
  • Your Spotify sign-in stays on your device. LyricDock's servers never see it.
  • Google sign-in only tells LyricDock your name, email address and profile picture, to let you into the apps. It isn't stored in a database.
  • The apps send anonymous usage data (no name, no email, no IP address), deleted after about three months.

Signing in with Google

The LyricDock apps (the Android app and the web app at app.lyricdock.losthusky.qzz.io) and the admin pages use Sign in with Google at auth.lyricdock.losthusky.qzz.io. The Android app opens that page in your phone's browser and comes back to the app when you're done.

  • What LyricDock receives: your name, email address, profile picture, Google account ID, and whether Google has verified the email address. These are the basic openid, email and profile permissions. LyricDock asks for nothing else: no access to Gmail, Drive, Contacts or any other Google data.
  • How it's used: only to let you into the apps and their album-art service, to show who is signed in, and to check that an account is allowed into the admin pages.
  • Where it's kept: in a signed cookie in your browser (__Secure-ld_sso) that expires 14 days after you last used LyricDock, and in a signed sign-in token in the app's storage on your device, which expires 90 days after you last used the app. LyricDock keeps no database of users. A second cookie (__Host-ld_st) lives for at most 10 minutes while you sign in, to protect the sign-in from forgery.
  • Sharing: none. Google user data is never sold, transferred to others, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

LyricDock's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Spotify

  • Following your Spotify account ("Sign in with Spotify"):
    • You sign in with your own Spotify developer app (your Client ID).
    • The sign-in tokens are stored only on your device, in the app or browser storage.
    • Requests go straight from your device to Spotify; LyricDock's servers are never involved.
    • LyricDock reads what is playing, your queue, recently played, library and playlists, and sends the controls you press (play, skip, volume, like). It does this only to show and control your music.
  • Following Spotify on your computer (the LyricDock extension):
    • Your computer and your screen talk directly to each other.
    • The short messages they exchange to find each other pass through a relay (ntfy or LyricDock's own copy of it), encrypted with your pairing code, so the relay only ever sees unreadable data.
    • When the two are on different networks, the connection may run through Cloudflare's relay (TURN). It only forwards the encrypted connection.

You can remove LyricDock's access to Spotify at any time at spotify.com/account/apps.

Anonymous usage data

The apps report a little usage data to LyricDock's cloud (art.lyricdock.losthusky.qzz.io), so it can count active screens, spot errors and run the album-art service:

A random device IDMade on your device, not linked to your name, email or accounts
App and deviceApp version, platform (Android, web, installed web app), device or browser type, screen size, time zone, language
EventsApp opened, installed or updated, an error (its message and code location only), a Spotify command that failed (which command and Spotify's reason)
Album-art lookupsThe artist and album names of songs whose cover is looked up
Approximate locationCountry, city and a map position rounded to about 10 km, worked out by Cloudflare from the connection. The IP address itself is not stored.

This data is kept for about three months and then deleted automatically. Because it can't be linked back to you, it can't be found or deleted on request before then.

Other services the apps contact

To do their job, the apps contact these services directly from your device. Each has its own privacy policy.

  • LRCLIB (lyrics): the song's title, artist, album and length.
  • Spicy Lyrics API (lyrics): only if you add your own key; the Spotify track ID.
  • Apple Music's public catalogue (album art and animated covers): reached through LyricDock's cloud with the artist and album names.
  • YouTube or Piped (music-video background): only if you choose that background; the song's title and artist.
  • Cloudflare and Google STUN servers: help two devices find a connection path.
  • GitHub: update checks and release notes.
  • Google Fonts: font files.

Data kept on your device

Your settings, the pairing code, your LyricDock and Spotify sign-in tokens, and saved lyrics and animated covers (for offline use and fewer downloads) stay in the app's or browser's storage on your device.

  • Clear the saved lyrics and covers in Settings → Storage.
  • Sign out of Spotify in Settings → Playback source.
  • Or remove everything by uninstalling the app or clearing the site's data in your browser.

Your choices

  • Sign out of LyricDock in Settings → Playback source → LyricDock account, or in a browser at auth.lyricdock.losthusky.qzz.io.
  • Remove LyricDock's access to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/connections.
  • For anything else, or to ask what LyricDock knows about you, email ghanisht.kumawat@gmail.com.

Security

All connections use HTTPS. Sign-in cookies are signed and can't be read by scripts. Pairing messages are end-to-end encrypted. No system is perfectly secure, but LyricDock keeps as little as it can, so there is little to lose.

Children

LyricDock is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes
